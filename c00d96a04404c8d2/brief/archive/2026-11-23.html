Space

  • Update, the Ariane 6 Amazon Leo launch is scrubbed: Arianespace stopped the VA271 countdown before the gantry was rolled back, after a "technical event" in the ground support systems. The rocket and its 36 satellites are safe, and the next attempt is no earlier than Tuesday, Nov. 24. Arianespace
  • Falcon 9 sets a 32-hour pad turnaround record: NROL-136 lifted off from Vandenberg's SLC-4E at 7:23 AM ET Sunday, 32 hours 3 minutes after USSF-317 flew from the same pad. The old record was 45 hours, set at the Cape's Pad 40. VP of launch Kiko Dontchev says the "next goal" is a 24-hour turn. It was the NRO's sixth launch of 2026. Space.com
  • States sue SpaceX over Grok deepfakes: The Oregon and Virginia attorneys general filed separate consumer-protection suits against SpaceX and X Corp. They allege the company misled users about Grok's safety and about how seriously it fought child sexual abuse material and nonconsensual intimate imagery. WIRED (paywall)
  • Update, BepiColombo's insertion burn ran short: ESA says a software issue cut the Mercury orbit-insertion burn short. The spacecraft is safe, ESOC teams are investigating, and an update is promised today. SpacePolicyOnline · Starlust
  • Update, Shenzhou-24 is nearly ready with Pakistan's first astronaut: China has finished a launch rehearsal, and Pakistan's planning minister attends the departure ceremony at Jiuquan today. One of two trained Pakistani candidates will spend about a week aboard Tiangong as its first foreign visitor. Arab News
